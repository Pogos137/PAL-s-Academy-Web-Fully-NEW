# Product

## Register

brand+product

## Users

**Primary (Brand):** Parents of high-school students in the Greater Toronto Area; university students seeking premium 1:1 tutoring in sciences, math, statistics.
- Context: Decision-makers (parents) seeking premium education investment; students seeking confidence + knowledge gap closure.
- Job to be done: Find trustworthy, premium tutoring; understand what success looks like; book a free consultation.

**Secondary (Product):** Students in active courses; tutors managing classes; academy admin overseeing enrollment, messaging, and student progress.
- Context: Weekly coursework, assignments, calendar planning, direct communication with tutors and peers.
- Job to be done: Track assignments, collaborate, access course materials, stay organized.

## Product Purpose

PAL's Academy is a **luxury 1:1 tutoring platform** that serves both a premium brand (acquisition & positioning) and a functional student portal + admin system (retention & operations).

- **Brand surface:** Position PAL's as premium, expert, empathetic. Drive leads through trust and clarity. Reinforce "built around the student, not a script."
- **Product surface:** Provide a classroom-like experience (Google Classroom aesthetic) where students manage assignments, calendar, and messages with their tutors.
- **Shared system:** One unified design language—elegant, premium, academic—that works across marketing pages, logged-in portal, and admin tooling.

Success = consistent brand signal from landing to login to coursework; conversion from visitor → lead → student; retention through a portal that feels purposeful and premium, not corporate.

## Brand Personality

**3 words:** Premium, Empathetic, Intentional.

**Voice & tone:** Calm, expert, unhurried. We speak to parents and students who value depth over quick fixes. Confident without hype. Honest about what tutoring can and can't do. We write as educators first.

**Emotional goals:**
- Build trust (expert credentials, clear communication, no false promises)
- Reduce anxiety (our approach is human-centered, not algorithm-driven)
- Create belonging (small cohorts, bespoke attention)

**Visual:** Emerald + gold palette signals luxury and academic tradition. Serif typography (Cormorant Garamond) conveys authority; sans (Inter) keeps copy accessible. Grain texture, motion, and glass effects add polish without feel sterile.

## Anti-references

**DO NOT look like:**
- Overly corporate SaaS (flat blues, rounded everything, generic card layouts)
- EdTech startup clichés (bright, playful, gamified, anxiety-inducing)
- Academic / utilitarian (cold, text-heavy, outdated)
- Luxury brands that feel distant or exclusionary (ostentatious, inaccessible)

**DO NOT use:**
- Fake urgency ("Spots filling fast," "Limited time," countdown timers)
- Stock photography of smiling students / tutors
- Every color in the rainbow
- Chatbots or AI chat as the primary interface

## Design Principles

1. **Premium through restraint, not excess** — emerald, gold, and ivory carry the brand. Motion is purposeful. Cards are minimal. Negative space is intentional.
2. **Expert confidence** — serif display type, generous spacing, clear hierarchy. No apologizing for simplicity.
3. **Humans, not algorithms** — copy highlights the tutor, the student, the relationship. Portal shows real people, real messages, real progress—not data abstraction.
4. **Consistency builds trust** — same color ramp, typography, spacing, component behavior across marketing, portal, and admin. A parent visiting the landing page should recognize the same aesthetic when their student logs in.
5. **Clarity over cleverness** — motion aids understanding, not distraction. Forms are straightforward. Errors are kind. No dark patterns.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

**WCAG Level AA minimum** (AAA for critical paths).

**Known requirements:**
- Reduced motion respected site-wide (no animation defaults if `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`)
- Color is never the only signal (links underlined, buttons have text + icon)
- Form inputs have clear labels and error messages
- Sufficient color contrast (gold on white tested; ensure inputs pass 4.5:1 on all backgrounds)
- Keyboard navigation: all interactive elements reachable via Tab/Enter/Escape
- Screen reader friendly: semantic HTML, ARIA labels for dynamic content (portal messages, calendar)

**Inclusion considerations:**
- Multi-language support (Canada: English + French should be planned)
- Clear, jargon-free copy where possible (tutoring-specific terms explained in context)
- Portal accessibility critical: students with ADHD, dyslexia, vision loss should navigate confidently
